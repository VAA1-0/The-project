# General Sprint core scientific closeout — 6 November 2026
## Governing conclusion

Datascene speaker attribution is science-grade at the authority boundary: model
outputs are reviewable proposals, while a source-linked analyst confirmation or
correction is canonical, reversible and projected across consumers. Suggestion
accuracy measures analyst workload and ranking quality; it does not determine
the validity of analyst-confirmed truth.

## Speaker evaluation

The current No Time to Die corpus contains 24 eligible confirmed audio seeds
across nine speaker labels. A leave-one-confirmed-seed-out test of the acoustic
cosine component produced 5/24 top-1 matches (20.83%). Margin abstention did not
improve accuracy. This component must therefore remain diagnostic and cannot
allocate speakers automatically.

That result is not the performance of Datascene's constellational design. The
Digital Twin constellation comprises visual evidence, audio/prosody, speaker
turns, movement, transcript, OCR, music/event context, Source Media data, Scene
Cards, measured lip synchrony, interaction evidence and manual confirmation.
The output-file hydration defect that previously hid several of these branches
has been repaired. The live artifact currently exposes 37 governed twins, with
35 visual, 27 audio, 27 speaker-turn, 27 transcript, 36 Scene Card, 21
music/event and 37 Source Media/manual-confirmation constellations. The saved
lip-synchrony artifact contains zero measurements and no governed movement rows
are present, so these are reported as unavailable rather than synthesized.
