## Statistical validity

The native statistical layer appropriately distinguishes robust standardized deviation from significance and rejects some nonconcurrent or single-family comparisons (`tests/test_native_statistical_interpretation.py`, lines 39–94). It does not by itself solve dependence among repeated frames, pseudo-replication, informative missingness, multiple testing, detector measurement error, or selection effects introduced by analyst confirmation. A method article should define video, scene, interval and occurrence sampling units before inferential claims and treat detector outputs as noisy measurements.

## Safe claims versus unsafe claims

Safe now: Datascene has tested provenance, authority, projection, invalidation and governed-reporting contracts; it integrates several pretrained detectors and transcript/visual evidence on a source-relative clock.

Unsafe now: Datascene autonomously learns Narrative Agents; assigns all spoken words correctly; resolves contradictions; validates Boje/SFL/behavioral constructs; achieves known detection accuracy; or reproduces every historical project without loss. Those require implementations or empirical studies not found in this audit.
