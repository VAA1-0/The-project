## Two interaction principles

The “saloon doors swing both ways” principle requires a correction initiated in Video to surface in the appropriate leaf panel, and a leaf-panel selection to seek and reveal the same source-time evidence in Video. The maturation and Video navigation implementations provide the mechanism (`src/frontend/components/panels/DataMaturationPanel.tsx`, lines 1556–2190; `src/frontend/components/panels/VideoPanel.tsx`, lines 5960–6118), while live-bus tests exercise cross-consumer refresh (`tests/test_live_mature_data_proliferation_bus.py`, lines 197–834). Its principal status is **IMPLEMENTED_NOT_ADEQUATELY_TESTED**, confidence **MEDIUM**, because the complete pairwise panel matrix is not demonstrated.

The “remain navigable within the leaf” principle means a raised indication retains a local row/card and local evidence action rather than forcing a context-destroying panel jump. Data Maturation implements aligned-panel opening and local decisions (`DataMaturationPanel.tsx`, lines 1556–1891). Its status is **PARTIALLY_IMPLEMENTED**, confidence **MEDIUM**: implementation evidence exists for key panels, not all leaf panels.

## Two runtime continuity principles

The **interactive annotation practice** makes analyst work the foreground
priority. The UI may update optimistically, but success means that the small
canonical correction artifact has been written atomically and verified. Master
Schema projection and other heavyweight consumers follow asynchronously; they
cannot delay or redefine the committed correction. This principle protects
both responsiveness and human analytical authority.

The **checkpoint and continuation practice** treats durable partial
measurements as governed evidence rather than disposable process residue.
Panels surface them with an explicit checkpoint/processing state. Continuation
must verify source identity and referenced artifacts, retain completed stages,
and resume from the durable position. Invalid checkpoints fail explicitly;
partial evidence never silently becomes completed evidence.
