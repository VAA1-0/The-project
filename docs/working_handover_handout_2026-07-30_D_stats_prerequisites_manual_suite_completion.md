## Governing product boundary

> Confirm once, project everywhere.

One valid user confirmation must enter one canonical governed decision and be consumed by every applicable panel, graph, timeline, search surface, report, export, and traceback view. Panel-local caches, detector outputs, tracking IDs, and local model iterations may not outrank or silently omit that decision.

The related operational rules are:

- manual correction wins;
- source time and source geometry remain occurrence-scoped;
- track IDs are traceback substrate, not identity;
- missing or untimed evidence must not be rendered at false time zero;
- detector confidence is not identity confidence;
- automatic matching must remain constellational, source-scoped, conflict-aware, and reversible;
- Master Schema stays inside every confirmation and proliferation loop;
- raw evidence remains available for traceback after maturation.
