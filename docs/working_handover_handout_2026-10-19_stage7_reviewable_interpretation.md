## Stepwise sprint

### Step 7.0 — Freeze the construct contract

Define a stable assertion identity consisting of project, analysis artefact,
source revision, construct type, subject, source interval and producer version.
Record the candidate/accepted/rejected/deferred/superseded states and prohibit a
second live assertion for the same semantic identity.

Gate: the construct has a schema, stable identity, authority hierarchy and
explicit dependency list. Repeated generation updates provenance/history rather
than manufacturing duplicate assertions.

### Step 7.1 — Assemble the eligible evidence envelope

Load the canonical Nomi audio occurrence, source clock and continuity projection
by revision. Include exact source anchors, modality, authority, maturity,
traceback references, missingness and freshness. Reject foreign-project, stale,
invalidated or panel-local substitutes.

Gate: every evidence item opens at the correct source interval and the envelope
names why each item is eligible. Missing prerequisites fail closed without
demoting the mature source confirmation.

### Step 7.2 — Generate a reviewable candidate

Create the bounded narrative-participation proposition as a candidate. Store the
producer and method versions, normalized effective settings and generation time.
The candidate must never overwrite evidence or present itself as an analyst
decision.

Gate: one eligible envelope produces one stable candidate; regeneration does not
create a second live assertion.

### Step 7.3 — Expose warrant, alternatives and uncertainty

Present the reasoning separately from the evidence. The initial warrant is that
an identity-confirmed, source-timed audio occurrence supports participation in
that beat. At minimum expose these alternatives:

- the utterance is Nomi's and constitutes participation in this beat;
- Nomi is referenced or represented, but is not the speaking participant;
- the audio identity is correct but the interpretive beat boundary is wrong;
- evidence is insufficient for this interpretive proposition.

Uncertainty belongs to the candidate interpretation, not to the already mature
user-confirmed occurrence.

Gate: the analyst can compare the main proposition and alternatives without any
option being silently promoted by confidence or display order.

### Step 7.4 — Deliver review inside the originating panel

Surface the complete review workflow inside the panel where the interpretation
is operationalized. Preserve source beat, scroll position, filters, queue
position, disclosures and drafts. Saloon-door synchronization may hydrate every
associated panel, but it must not move the analyst elsewhere. Cross-panel opening
is explicit and optional.

Gate: the analyst can review a sequence of candidates without panel switching or
loss of cognitive position.

### Step 7.5 — Record the governed analyst decision

Provide Confirm, Revise, Reject and Defer. Confirmation is an explicit user
confirmation and therefore commits the proposition immediately as 100% mature
Master Schema data. Revision creates the analyst-authored proposition and
supersedes the candidate. Rejection and deferral remain traceable and cannot
proliferate as mature truth.

Gate: the backend commits one canonical decision before any panel renders a
mature projection; the UI shows a durable save receipt.

### Step 7.6 — Project through both saloon doors

Publish the committed assertion revision to every applicable consumer, including
Narrative Agent, Meaning/Plot, Master Schema, Data Maturation, Search, Traceback,
Scene Cards and report selection. Audio and Video retain reciprocal source
navigation. No consumer asks the analyst to reconfirm the same assertion.

Gate: all applicable consumers report the same assertion ID and revision, while
the originating panel retains focus and working state.

#### Step 7.6A — Materialize graph nodes and editable relation tickets

A generic consumer receipt is not sufficient graph delivery. The confirmed
audio occurrence must materialize as the same governed graph substrate in both
the Meaning Knowledge Graph and Narrative Agent Graph:

- one Narrative Agent node for Nomi;
- one source-occurrence node for the audio interval;
- one narrative-beat node for the bounded interpretive beat;
- the containing scene/situation node when a governed scene exists; and
- source-linked relation edges between those nodes.

Every relation edge must open an editable in-panel account/ticket using the
existing Meaning Network annotation-sheet grammar. The ticket contains stable
edge and assertion IDs, subject, predicate and object, editable analyst meaning,
candidate/confirmed/rejected/deferred/superseded state, evidence and
counter-evidence, warrant, alternatives, uncertainty, producer provenance,
source interval, scene/situation scope, dependency revisions, traceback and
correction history. Confirm, Revise, Reject and Defer are governed decisions,
not cosmetic graph styling.

The audio confirmation may directly support identity/source-presence edges. It
may propose—but cannot automatically mature—interpretive edges such as
`participates_in`, `addresses`, `responds_to`, `aligns_with` or
`contradicts`. The two graphs are synchronized projections of one canonical
relation ticket; editing either graph must not create a second edge or a second
authority.

Gate: the source occurrence is visibly located in both graphs; every projected
edge opens its local editable ticket; saving once returns one canonical revision
to both graphs; and neither graph asks for duplicate confirmation.

#### Step 7.6B — Assemble governed constellational snapshots

A constellational snapshot is a source-bounded explanatory view, not a flattened
AI summary. It answers, with separately governed layers:

1. **Who is present?** Confirmed agent-presence and source-occurrence evidence.
2. **Who is in dialogue?** Speaker assignments, turns, overlap and addressee or
   response relations; co-presence alone must not be labelled dialogue.
3. **What are they talking about?** Source-linked transcript propositions,
   entities and topic candidates with alternatives and uncertainty.
4. **What is the scenic and situational context?** Scene boundaries, setting,
   objects, actions, roles and relevant preceding/following state.
5. **How does bodily expression relate to what is said?** Time-aligned pose,
   gesture, movement, facial-expression and prosody evidence. Correlation or
   temporal alignment must not be labelled cause, intention or emotion without
   a separately reviewed interpretive assertion.

Each layer reports `confirmed`, `candidate`, `conflicted`, `missing` or
`not applicable`; it never fills a missing layer with plausible prose. Every
sentence, chip and graph relation returns to its exact source anchor and opens
the same editable relation ticket. The snapshot preserves independent timing
where utterance, reaction and bodily expression overlap only partially.

The first Nomi snapshot may therefore truthfully show a confirmed Nomi audio
occurrence while leaving dialogue partner, topic, scene role or bodily response
as candidate/missing until their specific evidence is eligible. Completeness is
not manufactured for visual symmetry.

Gate: a snapshot survives save/reopen, reconstructs from the same canonical
revision, exposes contradictions and missingness, navigates every populated
layer locally, and updates selectively when one dependency is corrected.

### Step 7.7 — Prove correction and dependency invalidation

Correct the proposition, its beat boundary or its supporting source link and
verify targeted recomputation. A source-evidence correction marks the dependent
interpretation stale or invalidated; it does not erase history or unrelated
assertions. A later explicit user correction outranks the earlier confirmation.

Gate: affected consumers converge on the corrected revision; stale projections
cannot remain report-eligible, and unaffected mature evidence is preserved.

### Step 7.8 — Prove persistence and local navigation

Save, close, restart and reopen the project. Restore the assertion, decision,
alternatives, warrant, source anchors, history and originating-panel queue
position. Test attached and detached panels and both directions of source
navigation.

Gate: semantic parity and project hermeticity hold after reopen; no foreign or
sparse projection outranks the canonical record.

### Step 7.9 — Prove governed export

Export the construct with its accepted/candidate/history distinction, evidence
chain, producer versions, analyst decision, canonical revision and checksums.
Recheck freshness and eligibility at export time. Restore it in isolation and
reproduce the visible assertion and source return.

Gate: the isolated restoration retains the same meaning and provenance without
flattening a candidate, rejection or superseded assertion into accepted truth.

### Step 7.10 — Close the slice and select the next construct

Record automated and rendered evidence, manual acceptance, analyst effort,
limitations and optional deferrals. Expand only after this complete vertical
slice passes. Select the next construct according to satisfied dependencies,
not according to the breadth of an existing schema or menu.

Gate: this construct may be marked operational. Stage 7 remains partial until
each selected construct independently passes the same lifecycle.
